Shipping

Cerhera Nails currently ships within the United States only. Flat-rate shipping is calculated at checkout, with free shipping once you pass the threshold set in the shop.

  • Processing: usually 1–3 business days
  • Transit: about 3–7 business days after handoff
  • You’ll receive tracking when your order ships

International shipping isn’t available in this first version — we’ll expand carefully later.
